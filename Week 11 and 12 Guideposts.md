Hope you all had a good weekend. We’re starting week 12!

By now, your team should be clear on your project’s use case, have fully dissected your model project, and have started implementation.

For implementation, focus on two things:

Deployment automation
Featureset decisions

Deployment Automation

Some teams prefer to wait until the end for this. That’s fine, but I’d suggest taking a stab at it early, even if your final architecture isn’t settled. Pick the core services your project will use and automate their deployment. Terraform is a popular choice, but CloudFormation or the AWS SDK are also worth a look.

Featureset Decisions

This used to be hard, and we often fell back on “whoever can make progress wins.” With Claude at our disposal, implementation risk is gone. That shifts the main work to deciding which features to build and why, how the DX/UX should work, and why we’re not building other features. The reasoning behind each decision now matters more than the implementation itself.

To decide what to build or skip, lean on your understanding of the use case and the model project. Be careful about inventing “wouldn’t it be nice” features. If you spot an opportunity that neither the model project nor its competitors have, be suspicious of it.

Tip: try having Claude work through mockups to explore features and DX before committing to anything.

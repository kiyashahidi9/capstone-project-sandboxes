More implementation tips:

use high-fidelity (eg, html) mockups to brainstorm DX and features. Use Claude to help with this. In the past, high fidelity mockups were the last check step before implementation and very few Capstone teams used high fidelity mockups at all. With AI, we can now experiment with complete end to end UIs before any implementation.
use the "grill me" skill to help flesh out design decisions. This skill is a lot, but pick and choose what makes sense for your project.
build a team knowledge base. Write short docs for various libraries or tools that you come across. Capstone design decisions made and not made. Draft notes about thoughts from teammates. This will become the raw material for your project writeup.

Clone vs Wrap
Once you've identified a model project and have fully understood its use case (problem space) and featureset (solution space), the next step is to determine if we are going to "clone" or "wrap" the model project.
Clone: typically this will be the open source version of an existing product (think: web application such as Github.com).
Wrap: the model project will be part of our project, but we will not be cloning it. Eg, it's Postgres to us. (think: git. We will not be cloning git, but instead be using git to build an open source Github.com)
The project proposal deliverable is meant to help your team consolidate ideas into a document. Don't stress about the format or meeting a word count requirement. Clarity above all; showcase your depth of understanding of the use case and model project. Don't make it overly salesy or persuasive. It should be a technical document.
If you have all the above set, the you are ahead of schedule. The step after that is thinking about implementation. Please note: most teams should not be thinking about that at this stage. But if you're already there, then we are looking for implementation paths. Historically implementation has not been a major issue, and with AI this should go even faster. I'd like to encourage you to do more research even on implementation and try to appeal to authority:
an established pattern (preferrably by someone well know like Fowler or Nygard or Kleppmann, etc)
similar implantation by another well known company (eg, company blog post or conference talk, etc)
looking at existing open source solutions
Feel free to also use prototyping with AI to help make progress and try different implementation approaches. As featuresets becomes less impressive, the scope and variance of work will become more important. And even the path you ended up not taking will be interesting during interviews.
